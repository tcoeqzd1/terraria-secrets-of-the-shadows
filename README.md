## 📖 About

🔹 **Terraria — Secrets of the Shadows** is a powerful tool built to help you uncover dark secrets hidden in Terraria's underground with new lore items, enemies, and boss encounters. Secrets of the Shadows adds a shadowy narrative layer to Terraria through discovered journals, corrupted enemies, and dark bosses.

🔸 Whether you're a seasoned professional or just getting started, **Terraria — Secrets of the Shadows** delivers a clean, intuitive experience packed with everything you need — right out of the box.
